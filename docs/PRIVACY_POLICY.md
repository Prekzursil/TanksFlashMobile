# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-09

This privacy policy applies to the **Tanks** app and related builds distributed from this repository.

## Summary

- We do not require account sign-in.
- We do not intentionally collect personal data.
- Gameplay and settings are stored locally on your device.

## Data We Process

The app may store the following on-device data for functionality:

- gameplay settings (for example controls, UI preferences)
- local save/configuration state
- optional local diagnostics exported by the user

This data remains on your device unless you choose to share exported files.

## Data We Do Not Collect

By default, this project does not include:

- analytics SDKs
- advertising SDKs
- remote user tracking
- server-side account profiles

If future versions add network services, this policy must be updated before release.

## Permissions

Platform permissions are limited to what is required for app operation (for example, network access where applicable).
No sensitive permissions are requested unless a future feature requires them.

## Third-Party Components

The app uses open-source components (for example Ruffle, Capacitor, Electron, Godot in remake track).
Third-party notices are listed in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

## Children’s Privacy

This project is not specifically directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your Choices

You can:

- clear local app data via platform settings
- uninstall the app to remove local storage
- choose whether to export/share diagnostics

## Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or data concerns:

- Support page: `docs/SUPPORT.md`
